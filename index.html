Availability

Available for day work and short-term contracts. I’m on site during the day, so messages are picked up at breaks and in the evening — send the details and I’ll come back to you the same night where I can.

What happens when you get in touch

  1. 01Send the job details — dates, site postcode and the support needed — using Request dates, or message on WhatsApp if that’s easier.
  2. 02I check the request at breaks and in the evening and come back with a confirmation, a proposed alternative date or rate, or a straight no.
  3. 03Dates are only booked once I’ve confirmed — you’ll get an email with the details and a calendar file to add it.

What I bring

My own tools, including power tools, plus PPE. Consumables and materials aren’t included in the day rate — supply them yourself, or I’ll pick them up and invoice them separately at cost.
